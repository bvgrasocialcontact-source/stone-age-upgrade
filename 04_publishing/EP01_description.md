What happens next? Spoiler: the first thing humanity learns from AI isn't how to fight. It's how to boil water.

📚 Real Facts used in this video
• Oldest known Homo sapiens fossils: ~300,000 years old (Hublin et al., Nature, 2017)
• Writing appeared ~5,000 years ago (Mesopotamian cuneiform, c. 3200 BCE)
• Farming began ~12,000 years ago (the Neolithic Revolution)
• English is ~1,500 years old (Old English, 5th century CE)
• AI assistants run on servers in data centers, not inside your phone
• Stone boiling: heating water with hot rocks is a real prehistoric cooking method
• Oldest known soap recipe: ~4,800 years old (Babylon, c. 2800 BCE)
• Hunter-gatherer child survival to age 15: roughly 55–60% (Gurven & Kaplan, 2007; Kaplan et al., 2000)
• Honey has been used on wounds for thousands of years and has antibacterial properties (Mandal & Mandal, 2011)
• Oldest known counting bone (Lebombo bone): ~43,000 years old
• Germ theory became accepted in the 1860s–1880s (Pasteur, Koch)
• The last mammoths died out ~4,000 years ago (Wrangel Island)
• Neanderthals disappeared ~40,000 years ago (Higham et al., Nature, 2014)
• Birds are living dinosaurs — yes, including the ostrich
• Claude Code reads a codebase, edits files, runs commands/tests and fixes failures (Anthropic, Claude Code documentation)
