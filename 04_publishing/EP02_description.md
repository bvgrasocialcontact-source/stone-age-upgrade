## Açıklama (İngilizce, YouTube'a aynen)

You wake up 300,000 years ago in the tall grass… and right next to you there's a car. Shiny. Red. Four doors. The logo is covered in mud. No roads, no gas stations, and your tribe thinks the steering wheel is a very round snake. What happens next? Ostrich races, the first traffic jam (40 carts and one confused mammoth), a 40 kg speeding ticket, flying cars… and a museum where the engine is still running.

📚 Real Facts used in this video
• Oldest known Homo sapiens fossils: ~300,000 years old (Hublin et al., Nature, 2017)
• Ostriches can sprint at about 70 km/h — the fastest birds on land (PBS Nature; Guinness World Records)
• Oldest wooden wheel ever found: the Ljubljana Marshes Wheel, ~5,150 years old (Guinness World Records)
• Karl Benz patented the first automobile in January 1886 (German Patent and Trade Mark Office, DPMA)
• In August 1888 Bertha Benz drove ~100 km from Mannheim to Pforzheim and bought fuel at a pharmacy in Wiesloch — often called the first filling station (Britannica; DPMA)
• The first traffic light was installed in London on 9 December 1868; it ran on gas and exploded a few weeks later (Smithsonian Magazine)
• First speeding fine: England, 28 January 1896 — 8 mph (≈13 km/h) in a 2 mph zone (Guinness World Records)
• Road crashes kill about 1.19 million people every year (WHO, Global status report on road safety 2023)
• Birds are living dinosaurs — yes, including the ostrich
