## Real Facts used (with sources → description)
1. Oldest known Homo sapiens fossils ~300,000 years old — Hublin et al., Nature 2017 (EP01 source).
2. Ostriches sprint up to ~70 km/h, fastest bird on land — PBS Nature "Ostrich"; Guinness World Records "Fastest flightless bird on land" (72 km/h).
3. Oldest wooden wheel found: Ljubljana Marshes Wheel, ~5,150 years old (c. 3150 BCE) — Guinness World Records "Oldest wheel"; Wikipedia "Ljubljana Marshes Wheel" (5,100–5,350 BP, VERA radiocarbon).
4. Wheeled vehicles appear ~5,400 years ago in Eurasia/Middle East — Discover Magazine (Bridget Alex).
5. Karl Benz patented the first automobile, Jan 1886 (Reichspatent 37435) — DPMA (German Patent and Trade Mark Office) "First car ride in the world".
6. Bertha Benz, Aug 1888: first long-distance drive (~104–106 km, Mannheim→Pforzheim) without telling her husband; bought fuel (ligroin, a cleaning solvent) at the town pharmacy in Wiesloch → "world's first filling station"; fixed a fuel line with a hat pin, leather brake linings — Britannica "Bertha Benz"; DPMA; Wikipedia "Bertha Benz Memorial Route".
7. Karl Benz received the world's first driving licence, 1 Aug 1888 — DPMA.
8. First traffic light: London, 9 Dec 1868, gas-lit, operated by a policeman; exploded 2 Jan 1869 (gas leak), injuring the officer — Wikipedia "History of traffic lights"; Smithsonian Magazine.
9. First speeding fine: Walter Arnold, 28 Jan 1896, Paddock Wood (Kent), 8 mph in a 2 mph zone (4× the limit), in a Benz — Guinness World Records "First person charged with a speeding offence". (Snopes: the "policeman on a bicycle" detail is uncorroborated → not used.)
10. Road traffic deaths ≈ 1.19 million per year worldwide — WHO Global status report on road safety 2023.
11. About 1.5 billion cars in the world (estimates 1.47–1.7 bn incl. trucks) — Hedges & Company; Jalopnik.
12. Birds are living dinosaurs (ostrich = "largest living dinosaur") — Wikipedia "Common ostrich".
